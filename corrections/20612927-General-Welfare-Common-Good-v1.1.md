## Work and earlier version

*“General Welfare” and “Common Good” as Evolutionary Attractors in Constitutional Law.*

Earlier version: [version 1.0, DOI 10.5281/zenodo.20612927](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.20612927). That version remains available as part of the version history.

## Correction

The earlier PDF incorrectly attributed the expression **bien común** to the Preamble of the Argentine Constitution and repeated that attribution in dependent passages. The Preamble states **“promover el bienestar general”**. Article 75(2), concerning taxation, uses the distinct wording **“bien general del Estado”**.

Version 1.1 corrects those attributions and distinguishes **bienestar general** as constitutional wording from **bien común** as a doctrinal and jurisprudential category. It removes the premise that the expressions necessarily have identical meanings or equivalent constitutional status, and revises the textualist comparison that depended on that premise. The comparison concerns distinct concepts, legal anchors and institutional uses.

The corrections affect the abstract, introduction, discussion and conclusion, including digital pages 1–4, 7, 11, 15, 20, 43–44 and 47. Two residual editorial instructions were also removed. The corrected PDF retains the 58-page sequence and is supplied in A4 format.

Official source: [Constitution of Argentina, official text, Preamble and Article 75(2)](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/normativa/nacional/804/texto).

## Scope

This is a correction to constitutional attribution and dependent argumentation. It does not report a new empirical analysis or independently revalidate the numerical results retained from version 1.0. Version 1.1 supersedes version 1.0 for the corrected passages; the earlier version is preserved for citation and historical transparency.
