## Immediate Decisions to Preserve

1. The dominant risk is validation asymmetry.
   - CLI and HBU can be described as data-validated where the paper cites the relevant evidence.
   - IHR, IEI, AIT/GIMT, PSO, DCF, SMD, LSE, holonomy, and TCI must not be described as equally validated unless the new paper supplies evidence.
   - Treat multipaís expansion as out-of-sample validation, not merely additional coverage.

2. Two hygiene decisions should be made before multipaís replication.
   - Fix the canonical CLI formula and explain any older four-component formulation as prior, alternate, or deprecated.
   - Resolve the latent TCI/CLI confusion: if TCI was once described as renamed from CLI 4.0, correct that language so TCI is treated as a distinct construct unless a paper expressly argues otherwise.

3. The highest-value bridge paper is AI as niche constructor of the legal memepool.
   - This is the preferred bridge between the institutional programme and the AI/alignment programme.
   - Write it in conceptual pair with Law as Constructed Niche when feasible.

4. The strongest rival-programme challenge is cultural attraction theory.
   - The response to develop is that law, because of canonical texts, professional transmission, citation practice, and formal writing, is an unusually strong domain for high-fidelity memetic replication.

## Mandatory Preflight for Future Papers

Before a future EPT/EGT paper is uploaded or treated as ready, verify the following:

1. Validation status is explicit.
   - Do not present IHR, IEI, AIT/GIMT, PSO, DCF, SMD, LSE, holonomy, or TCI as empirically validated unless the paper supplies independent evidence.
   - If an instrument is theoretical, simulated, calibrated, or in validation, say so plainly.
   - Preserve the distinction between verified data, reasoned inference, and conjecture.

2. CLI language is disciplined.
   - Use one canonical CLI formula in a given paper.
   - If an older/four-component CLI appears, identify it as prior or alternate and explain the relation.
   - Do not blur CLI and TCI: CLI is structural rigidity; TCI is memetic transmission capacity unless a paper expressly redefines the terms.

3. Claims of a diagnostic system are not overstated.
   - If CLI, IHR, IEI, and HBU point in the same direction, do not claim construct differentiation without evidence.
   - Prefer at least one off-diagonal case where instruments diverge and the divergence is informative.

4. The paper either validates, bridges, or clearly remains theoretical.
   - Rule of discipline: for each new instrument-like construct, pair the publication pipeline with validation of an existing instrument.
   - If no empirical validation is added, state the paper's contribution as theoretical architecture, not validated measurement.

5. The Dawkins-Gould / levels-of-selection issue is handled when relevant.
   - Papers using Dawkinsian memetics plus Gouldian spandrels/exaptation should specify what does causal work.
   - Use Hull's replicator/interactor/vehicle distinction where it clarifies the level of selection.

6. Niche construction is treated as a central bridge, not an ornament.
   - Future IA/legal-AI papers should ask whether AI systems are constructing the memetic niche through which legal norms replicate.
   - Future institutional papers should ask how today's legal phenotype modifies tomorrow's selective environment.

7. Rival programmes are engaged in their strongest form when the paper touches their domain.
   - Cultural attraction theory: explain why law can still be a high-fidelity memetic domain because of writing, canonical texts, and professional transmission.
   - EGT/legal theory: consider Binmore, Young, Bowles-Gintis when justice, conventions, cooperation, or game-theoretic law are central.
   - Path dependence: position CLI against recognized path-dependence literature when appropriate.
   - Fitness landscapes: use Kauffman/NK models when the paper relies on rugged landscapes, epistasis, or Mount Improbable metaphors.

8. Falsifiability is operational, not decorative.
   - Where possible, preregister or timestamp at least one prediction before the outcome is known.
   - Report failed or ambiguous predictions when they occur.
   - Avoid a Lakatosian declaration that never exposes the protective belt to risk.

9. Normative/prescriptive claims require an intervention theory.
   - If a paper diagnoses lock-in, zombie law, or parasitic spontaneous order, ask: what intervention, mutation, shock, or niche modification could move the equilibrium?
   - If the answer is not developed, state that the paper is diagnostic only.

10. Citation hygiene is mandatory.
   - Use Zenodo DOIs, not SSRN as the authoritative citation base.
   - Remove placeholder DOIs such as `10.5281/zenodo.XXXXXXX` before upload.
   - Remove obsolete SSRN working-paper references unless retained only as historical metadata.

11. Terminology is controlled.
   - Use `hysteretic`, not `hysterical`.
   - Use `falsable`, not `falsificable`, in Spanish programme terminology.
   - Define PSO as convergence without coordination, not coordination without coordination.

12. Prioritize papers that consolidate the programme.
   - High-priority bridges: AI as niche constructor of the legal memepool; Law as Constructed Niche; Hull replicator/interactor/vehicle hygiene; Dawkins-Gould adjudication; validation of IHR/IEI; off-diagonal instrument cases; cultural-attraction defense; theory of reform windows.

## Upload Gate

A paper can be uploaded to Zenodo if it is mature enough on its own terms, but the upload note or metadata review should classify it as one of:

- Empirical validation
- Theoretical architecture
- Bridge paper
- Methodological correction
- Normative/intervention module
- Positioning against rival programme

If the paper introduces a new instrument, record what existing instrument or prediction will be validated next.
